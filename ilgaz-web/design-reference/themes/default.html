§ 04 — About

Short biography.

Edited annually. Occasionally honest.
Somewhere off Fountain Square, 2025

I grew up between Baku and a grandmother's garden in Quba, which is probably why I like software that feels lived-in. I studied computer science, drifted through a few companies, and now I make small things on the side and write about the process in public.

This website is the commonplace book — a slow, hand-maintained index of what I'm reading, building, and trying to figure out. It has no newsletter, no analytics that matter, and no opinions it isn't willing to walk back.

If you want to get in touch, I answer email within about a week. If you want to see what's new, the RSS feed is the only reliable way.

Thank you for reading to the bottom — it means more than the analytics would ever suggest.
