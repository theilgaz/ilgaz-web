The vibe

You asked for: Win98, Win XP, Terminal, 90s web. That's four different retro flavors — they all share "visible chrome, low resolution, no minimalism" but they're aesthetically distinct. Plan is to sketch all four side-by-side first, pick a winner (or a mash-up), then go hi-fi.
